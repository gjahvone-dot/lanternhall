Getting started

Pick a world on the Worlds page and tap Make a character here. You'll choose a name, shape their face and body, say where they come from, describe how they look (the AI draws them from your words), say where they come from, and pick one of that world's classes. The Forge rolls their abilities and writes a backstory while you play. Tap Begin story to play solo, or Play with friends instead to open or join a table.

Taking a turn

Type what your character does in the box under the story and press Act. Write actions in plain words and speech in quotes:

I lean on the bar and slide a coin over. "Who runs the night market?"

The Game Master answers with what happens next. Suggested actions appear as buttons, but you can always do something else. The Game Master never decides what your character feels or says.

Dice and checks

When an action could fail, the Game Master asks for a check, such as Dexterity, DC 14. Press the roll button: you roll a d20 and add your modifier. Meet or beat the DC to succeed. A natural 20 always succeeds spectacularly; a natural 1 always goes wrong.

You can also roll any skill from the Skills tab. Class skills (marked with a dot) add a proficiency bonus of +2, rising as you level.

Your character

  • HP drops when you're hurt and recovers when you rest or heal. At 0 you fall unconscious.
  • XP comes from overcoming challenges, clever ideas, great roleplay and finished quests. Every level raises your max HP.
  • Inventory holds gold and gear the Game Master hands out. Equip weapons and armor, or use consumables.
  • Abilities come from your class. Tap one in the Skills tab to use it.

Quests

Your first quest usually arrives in the opening scene. For more, open the Quests tab and visit the quest board: three jobs written for where you are right now, each with a giver, a reward and a danger level. Take one and the Game Master weaves it into the story. Objectives tick off as you go, and finishing a quest earns +20 XP.

People and dialogue

Everyone you meet shows up under Relationships, with how they feel about you: Hostile, Wary, Curious, Friendly or Loyal. Open a card to talk to that person directly. Important lines appear as speech cards with the speaker's face; press play to hear them, or turn on Read dialogue aloud in the Game Master tab.

The AI council

Four AIs share the work. The Game Master narrates. The Chronicler keeps the journal, quests and relationships, and moves the Fate meter from Ruin to Mercy based on how your party behaves. The Voices speak for characters. The Forge builds classes, backstories and quests.

The Game Master tab has shortcuts: Catch me up, Fix a memory (correct something the story got wrong), Rewrite the story (redo the last turn), Explain a rule, and more. You can also ask the Game Master anything out of character.

Playing with friends

Choose Open a table and share the five-letter code. Friends go to Characters, pick who they're playing, enter the code and press Join. Everyone sees the same story, rolls and party. The person who opened the table runs the Game Master; if they leave, anyone can take over.

Tips

  • Be specific. "I check the lock for scratches" beats "I look around".
  • Talk to people. Named characters remember how you treated them.
  • Failure is interesting. A bad roll moves the story somewhere new.
  • Use Fix a memory if the story forgets something important.
